## Description (4000 chars max)

Mooring Booking is the Mediterranean's #1 marketplace for discovering and booking private and commercial mooring spots across Croatia, Greece, Italy, Spain, Montenegro, and beyond.

**Find Your Perfect Mooring**
Search thousands of verified moorings by location, dates, boat size, and amenities. Compare prices, read genuine reviews, and book instantly — no phone calls required.

**Key Features:**
• 🗺️ Interactive map view with GPS coordinates
• 📅 Real-time availability calendar
• ⭐ Verified reviews from real sailors
• 💳 Secure card payments via Stripe
• 🤖 AI Captain — your personal nautical assistant powered by AI
• 🔔 Instant booking confirmations and status updates
• ❤️ Save favourite moorings for quick access
• 📖 Sailing Handbook — 11 articles on safety, navigation & weather

**For Mooring Owners**
List your private mooring or marina berths and earn from underused spaces. Manage availability, track bookings and revenue from the dashboard. Get paid directly via Stripe.

**Why Mooring Booking?**
Unlike VHF radio or harbour master queues, Mooring Booking lets you plan ahead. Browse real photos, GPS locations, and honest reviews before you arrive. Our AI Captain answers navigation questions and helps you find the best spot for your itinerary.

**Destinations:**
Croatia · Greece · Italy · Spain · Montenegro · Slovenia · France · Turkey · Malta · Cyprus

**Safe & Secure**
All moorings are verified. Payments are processed by Stripe. Your data is protected under GDPR.
