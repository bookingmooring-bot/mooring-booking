# Windlass Troubleshooting — Brand-Specific Guide

## Common Marine Windlass Brands
- Maxwell (most common on Mediterranean yachts)
- Lofrans (Italian, popular on charter yachts)
- Quick (Italian)
- Lewmar (British)
- Muir (Australian)
- Italwinch
- Imtra / Vetus

---

## Maxwell Windlass

**VWC / RC Series (most common)**

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Nothing happens | Blown circuit breaker (reset) or main fuse | Reset breaker; replace fuse only with same rating |
| Hum, won't move | Chain jam under gypsy OR thermal protection tripped | Clear jam; wait 10 min for thermal reset |
| Works only up OR only down | Solenoid pack fault or control button | Test buttons with multimeter, replace solenoid |
| Chain slips on gypsy | Wrong chain calibration OR worn gypsy | Recalibrate or replace gypsy to match chain size |
| Smoke from motor | Motor overloaded (chain too long, anchor stuck) | Stop immediately — 10-min rest minimum |

**Clutch disengagement (Maxwell):**
- Unscrew the chrome/black clutch handle on top of windlass (counter-clockwise)
- Do NOT use electric motor with clutch disengaged
- Re-engage by screwing clockwise until snug

---

## Lofrans Windlass

**Tigres / Poseidon / Project / Cayman Series**

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Nothing, relay clicks | Check for thermal protection (auto-resets after 10 min cool-down) |
| Gypsy slipping | Adjust gypsy chain calibration bolt under gypsy |
| Motor overheats | Typical: chain jam or too many continuous operations — rest motor |

**Lofrans Clutch Release:**
- Flat hex key (often 6mm) in center of gypsy — rotate 90° to disengage
- Some models: chrome thumb screw

---

## Lewmar Windlass

**EVO / Pro-Series / Ocean Series**

- Self-resetting thermal breaker (usually 10–15 min wait)
- Windlass fuse is typically in a waterproof fuse holder at battery
- Control panel uses standard marine bus cable — easy to test with multimeter

**Calibration:** Lewmar gypsies are chain-size specific — mixing chain sizes causes slipping.

## General Windlass Maintenance Schedule

| Task | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Grease gypsy bearings (marine waterproof grease) | Every season |
| Inspect chain for stretch, twist, rust | Every season |
| Check all cable connections — especially high-amp cable at motor | Every season |
| Test manual clutch disengagement | Every season |
| Check chain stopper / devil's claw | Before passage |
| Flush with fresh water | After every saltwater use |

---

## Emergency Hand Retrieval (No Power)

1. Disengage clutch manually
2. Put a snubber/bridle on chain first — take weight off windlass
3. Pull chain hand-over-hand using a thick leather glove
4. Pile chain on deck neatly — don't let it pile in one spot (can capsize small boats)
5. Once anchor aboard, re-engage clutch and restore power

## Windlass Cable Sizing Reference

| Windlass Rating | Recommended Cable Size |
|---|---|
| Up to 500W / 40A | 16mm² (AWG 5) |
| 500–1000W / 80A | 25mm² (AWG 3) |
| 1000–2000W / 160A | 50mm² (AWG 1) |

**Rule:** Keep runs short. Every extra metre of cable causes voltage drop, reducing windlass power significantly.
