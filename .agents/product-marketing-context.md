## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Mooring Booking is the Mediterranean's #1 marketplace for private mooring and berth bookings.
**What it does:** Connects private mooring owners (providers) with sailors/boaters looking for safe, affordable berths across the Mediterranean. Providers list their moorings with photos, GPS coordinates, availability calendars, and pricing. Guests browse, book instantly, and navigate to their berth. The platform handles reservations, reviews, and commission-based payouts.
**Product category:** Maritime / Nautical Booking Marketplace (like "Airbnb for moorings")
**Product type:** Two-sided marketplace (SaaS + commission model)
**Business model:**
- **Providers:** Free to list. 15% commission on successful bookings. Optional paid add-ons: Marketing Tools (€5/mo), Premium Listing (€9.99/mo), Mooring Insurance (€9.99/yr).
- **Sailors/Guests:** Free tier (Basic Captain). Premium Monthly (€19.99/mo) and Premium Annual (€9.99/mo billed yearly) for advanced features like AI Captain, priority booking, and weather forecasts.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Mediterranean mooring owners have unused capacity sitting idle — no easy, trustworthy way to monetize it without word-of-mouth or local connections.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- No dedicated marketplace exists for private moorings (marinas have their own systems but private owners have nothing)
- Facebook groups and local classified ads are fragmented, unreliable, and unprofessional
- Direct marina booking systems don't accept private buoys or individual berths
- Word-of-mouth limits reach to local sailors only
**What it costs them:** Thousands of euros in lost seasonal income. A single mooring in Dubrovnik could earn €6,000+ per season.
**Emotional tension:** Frustration watching empty moorings while knowing there's demand. Fear of letting strangers use their property without a platform to manage trust, reviews, and payments.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** No true direct competitor exists for a private mooring marketplace at Mediterranean scale. This is a first-mover opportunity.
**Secondary:** Marina booking platforms (e.g., Navily, Dockwa) — but they serve commercial marinas, not private owners.
**Indirect:** Facebook sailing groups, local harbormaster offices, word-of-mouth — fragmented, no payments, no calendar management, no reviews.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Only platform purpose-built for **private** mooring owners (not just commercial marinas)
- 11 Mediterranean countries in one marketplace with 15-language support
- Free to list with simple 15% commission — no upfront costs, no subscriptions required
- GPS-powered interactive map with real-time weather data
- Calendar-based availability management with custom daily pricing
- Built-in review system builds trust over time
- QR code & affiliate link system for providers to market their own moorings
- AI Captain assistant for sailors
- Now4Today same-day booking feature
**How we do it differently:** Zero barriers to entry. A mooring owner can list in 10 minutes with photos, set their price, and get bookings from international sailors.
**Why that's better:** No financial risk. You only pay when you earn. The platform handles trust (reviews), reach (15 languages, SEO), and management (calendar, bookings).
**Why customers choose us:** "I was earning nothing from my mooring. Now I earn €5,000+ per season with zero upfront cost."
