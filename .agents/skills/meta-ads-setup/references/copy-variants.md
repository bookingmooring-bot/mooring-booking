## Copy Guidelines

- All copy in **English only** (all 10 Mediterranean markets)
- Use `--` not em dash `—` (API encoding compatibility)
- Hook = attention-grabber, max 125 chars
- Body = 1 of 3 variants, rotated to prevent fatigue
- CTA text = action-oriented, urgency, free emphasis
- Headline = max 40 chars (Meta limit for headlines)
- Description = max 30 chars (Meta limit)
- Landing page: `https://mooring-booking.com/become-provider`
